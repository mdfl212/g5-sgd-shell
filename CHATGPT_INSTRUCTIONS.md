## Visual and content rules

- Prefer flowcharts and rounded cards over text walls. One idea per block.
- Preserve original aspect ratio of figures; never stretch.
- Draw arrows after nodes so they remain on top.
- Do not merge unrelated cases.
- Treat source material as authoritative for case facts. Do not invent patient details, lab values, citations, or references.
- Use Vancouver numbered references; short in-slide cites such as 1,2 may be used.
- Default roster: BISTER, DEIPARINE, DATO, KANASHIRO, MONTEROSO, & TIBURON.
- Uploaded PowerPoints can be used to extract visual patterns, but must not override the locked spine or branding.
