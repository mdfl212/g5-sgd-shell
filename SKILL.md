# G5 SGD Shell

Portable workflow for producing **Group 5 Small Group Discussion (SGD)** decks as editable `.pptx` for modules **MD201–MD207**.

## When to use

- The user asks for an SGD deck, Group 5 presentation, or MD module case presentation.
- A case vignette, PDF, or notes are provided for a G5-style PowerPoint.
- The user asks to rebuild a shell/template or names MD201–MD207 with case content.

## Locked 8-step narrative spine

1. **Cover** — official SoM logo, university text, topic title, Group 5 roster.
2. **Case / Snapshot** — short vignette card and 2–3 tag chips.
3. **Guide Questions overview** — numbered list of all questions.
4. **Answer blocks** — one slide or card set per question; prefer cards and tables over prose.
5. **Tables / Flowcharts** — comparisons or decision algorithms where useful.
6. **Summary & Take-aways** — 3–5 high-yield points.
7. **References** — numbered Vancouver-style citations.
8. **Thank-you** — group roster and closing.

Extra slides may live inside a step, but never reorder or replace the top-level spine.

## Branding on every slide

- Official CSU SoM shield; do not stretch or crop it incorrectly.
- Header: CARAGA STATE UNIVERSITY / SCHOOL OF MEDICINE / CSU-Main Campus, Ampayon, Butuan City · Competence Service Uprightness.
- Course code at top-right.
- Three-segment subject-color bar at top and footer.
- Footer topic or short citation and page number.
- Cambria throughout. Cover title 34–40 pt; question 22–26 pt; body/table 12–14 pt.

## Visual and content rules

- Prefer flowcharts and cards over paragraph walls; one idea per visual block.
- Preserve source figure aspect ratios and never stretch images.
- Draw pathway arrows after boxes so they remain visible above nodes.
- Do not merge unrelated cases into one deck.
- Do not invent patient facts, lab values, clinical claims, or references. Mark gaps explicitly.
- Keep content traceable to source material and use Vancouver-style numbered references.
- Use person-first language for weight and stigma topics.
- Default roster: BISTER, DEIPARINE, DATO, KANASHIRO, MONTEROSO, & TIBURON.

## Output and review

Provide an editable PPTX when the environment supports it. Review the rendered slides, inspect overflow and source fidelity, and report limitations honestly. Structural validation alone does not prove clinical correctness or visual quality.
